Keep inline scripts
Preserve inline JavaScript from the original page. This is risky and may re-enable paywalls or trackers. Off by default.

Fetch as search engine
Re-fetch articles with a search engine crawler identity to retrieve full article content behind server-side paywalls. Disable if articles appear broken or show bot-verification pages.
Auto-bypass on known paywall sites
Automatically apply the paywall bypass when you visit any site in the built-in rules database. The bypass runs ~1.5 s after the page loads. Disable if you experience unexpected layout changes.
Block ads & trackers
Drop known ad, analytics, and tracker requests at the network level and hide ad slots on the page. Runs at page start for cleaner browsing and cleaner archive snapshots. On by default.
